✨ Our promise: We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our tools.

1. Who We Are

Queer Convert (operating at trinka.space) is a collection of free online tools including unit converters, image converters, and calculators. We are committed to creating a safe, inclusive space for everyone, especially the LGBTQ+ community.

For any privacy-related questions, you can contact us at: privacy@trinka.space

2. What Data We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. Here's what we collect and why:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Cookies & Tracking Cookie IDs, IP address, browser type, pages visited To show relevant ads and analyze site traffic
Usage Data Which tools you use, conversion history (stored locally only) To improve our tools and user experience
Ad Data Ad interactions, device identifiers To serve personalized ads via AdSense
Images Images you convert (processed in your browser only) JPG to PNG conversion - we NEVER upload or store your images
